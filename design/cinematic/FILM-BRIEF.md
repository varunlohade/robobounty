# CrashLab concept film: "You only get one try"

Concept film for the site and the demo opening. It is AI-generated fiction. It is NOT product footage, and our simulator did not make it or test this robot. Label it "Concept film" on the site, and disclose the tool and prompts in AI-USE.md.

Niche: a robot arm retrieving melted fuel debris inside a damaged nuclear reactor. Humans cannot enter, a robot that fails inside may never come out, and you cannot rehearse on the real thing. Fictional site, no real plant, company, logo, flag or readable text.

Story: one robot goes where nobody can follow. It reaches. It almost fails. Then: thousands of test worlds where it already failed, safely.

## Look
Anamorphic 2.39:1 framing, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, practical lights only, haze in the air, cold steel and concrete with one warm work light. Real film grain. No lens-flare spam, no neon, no sci-fi UI, no text in frame, no people's faces.

## The robot (same words in every prompt; generate a reference still first and reuse it)
A slender, jointed, industrial robot arm, about 6 metres long, on a rail, built of scuffed white and grey metal with exposed cables, a small work lamp and a camera at the wrist, and a simple two-finger gripper. Worn and practical, not shiny.

## Shots (about 8 s each, 45 s in total)
1. Darkness, then a heavy steel hatch slides open. Dust falls through a single beam of light. Slow push-in.
2. Wide: the arm unfolds along a rail into a flooded, ruined concrete chamber. Very small in frame. Slow lateral move.
3. Close on the wrist camera and lamp, passing bent pipes and hanging cables. Macro, shallow focus. Dust moving in the light.
4. The gripper descends to a dark, rough lump of debris on a ruined metal grate. Extreme close-up. It closes.
5. The grip slips by a few centimetres. The debris shifts and a fine dust rises. The arm freezes. Long, tense hold.
6. The same chamber turns to clean simulator-like geometry, then one view splits into many small copies of the scene, each slightly different, some ending in failure. Pull back. White fades in.

End card, added in editing, not by the AI: "You only get one try." Then the CrashLab name. Palatino on white.

## Site use
A 10-12 s silent loop from shots 2-4 near the top of the site, under a "Concept film" label, with a click-through to the full film. Sound stays off unless a viewer turns it on.
