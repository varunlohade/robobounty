# Film brief for Astra

Owner's words: "You can have a robot that cleans mirrors on a high-rise building ... tell it to describe the video and tell it to make a video similar to that but for cleaning mirrors on a high-rise building ... that's the intro video we want."
Also his standing rule: "it should not be a direct copy, it should be an adaptation from them, but it should look significantly different in terms of looks of people don't think we copy".

## Step 1: describe
frames/ holds stills from a reference company's films: hero-*.jpg (a 4.2 s site hero loop at 1 frame per second) and run-*.jpg (a 112 s product film at 1 frame per 4 seconds). Write DESCRIPTION.md: what happens shot by shot, the camera moves, the lighting, what makes it feel real, and why it works as a site intro. You see stills, not motion, so mark anything about motion as inferred.

## Step 2: write our film
FILM.md: an adaptation for a robot that cleans the glass of a high-rise tower. About 45 s, plus a 4-6 s hero loop for the top of the site. The film keeps the same emotional pull, but it must look significantly different: different robot, setting, colour, shots and order. Avoid their orange-to-teal look, underwater setting, hull setting, and any shot composition that matches theirs. Story for us: the robot works alone, high above a city. Wind. A near miss. Nobody can catch it up there. Then many safe test worlds where it already failed.
For each shot, write a ready-to-use text-to-video prompt, with one consistent description of the robot. No text, logos, real buildings, real brands, or recognisable people's faces in frame.

## Step 3: make what you can
Use your imagegen skill to make the key still for up to 6 shots, saved as keyframes/shot-1.png onwards, in 16:9, photoreal, film-like. Make the first one the robot reference and reuse its design. Hard limit: 8 image generations in total. Do not sign up for anything, buy anything, or use any video service. State honestly whether you have any way to make real video here.

Write only in this folder. Do NOT use Sites/hosting or publish tools. Never pay, send, submit, delete, publish or push. Keep working until DESCRIPTION.md, FILM.md and keyframes exist; do not stop after describing a plan. End with 3 lines: WHAT, WHY, CAN IT SCALE.
